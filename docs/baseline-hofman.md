# Frozen Hofman Baseline

The frozen Hofman baseline is the accounting reference of the repository. It is generated by `codes/run_baseline_hofman_1980.py` and published in additive 1980 CLP space.

## What it does

- Uses Hofman gross investment as the flow input.
- Uses observed Hofman gross and net capital stocks for 1950-1994 as overlap observations.
- Recovers implied retirements and depreciation from stock-flow identities in the overlap.
- Fits Weibull survival functions by asset.
- Anchors pre-1950 reconstructed stocks to the observed 1950 levels.
- Extends the capital gap through asset-specific `B/Kg` ratios outside the observed overlap.

## What is established from committed outputs

`outputs/assumptions_baseline_1980.yaml` records the survival family as Weibull for `ME`, `NRC`, and `RC`, and states that the pre-1950 anchor is a multiplicative factor that matches observed `Kg(1950)` by asset.

`outputs/overlap_audit_summary.txt` records zero negative implied retirements and zero negative implied depreciation for `Total` in the 1950-1994 overlap.

`outputs/audit_summary_full_1900_1994.txt` records seven total retirement-rate out-of-bounds observations over the full 1900-1994 horizon. In repository terms, the frozen baseline is therefore an overlap-coherent accounting reference, not a full-period admissibility benchmark with zero early warnings.

## Why it remains frozen

The promoted harmonized baseline reuses the frozen baseline as a reference object, not as a target for rewriting. In the current repository design, the frozen Hofman series supplies:

- the accounting benchmark
- the survival parameter source
- the net-to-gross gap-ratio source
- the asset-share source for the promoted harmonized split
